Decision engine

Money is never decided by a raw LLM.

The first hard verdict wins. Deterministic rules catch obvious failures, Nemotron handles bounded judgment inside the limits, and the owner resolves ambiguous cases by phone.

Margin rules Bounded JSON judgment Owner tap
three-layer governance
1
Deterministic rules

Duplicate invoices, unapproved payees, instruction overrides, over-budget self-spend, and margin-killing purchases are refused without model discretion.

2
NVIDIA Nemotron

Nuanced delivery-spend judgments are strict, bounded, and advisory unless the rules route the request onward.

3
Owner approval

Thin-margin or weak-evidence cases park the agent until a real human says yes or no.
